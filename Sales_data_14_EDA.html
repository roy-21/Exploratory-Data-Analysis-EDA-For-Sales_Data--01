Summary & Recommendation(2014 Sales):-The 2014 sales analysis reveals that Richard was the top-performing salesperson, based on both total sales and a custom Sales Score metric that accounts for order volume. Monthly trends show peak sales in January (5.06M), December (4.09M), and March (4.04M), while May, June, and November experienced the lowest sales. The correlation between discount and sales is weak (-0.0361), indicating that blanket discounts are ineffective. However, targeted discounts of 6–7% on ‘Low’ and ‘Not Specified’ priority orders may improve results. Shipping analysis shows Regular Air is most used, while Delivery Truck and Jumbo containers are the most expensive. In November 2015, launch a “Winter Discount Campaign” offering 6–7% discounts on low-priority orders. Pair this with cost-effective shipping methods like Regular Air and Small Boxes to reduce delivery expenses. This targeted approach will help boost sales in slow months while preserving profitability.
